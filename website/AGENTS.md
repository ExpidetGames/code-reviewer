# AGENTS.md (website/)

The code-review.com landing page: an Astro site built from the Itegro template (`Itegro-GmbH/landingpage-vorlage`,
version in `package.json` under `itegroVorlage`). Hosting, tracking and PostHog follow the process "Landingpages"
(`prozesse/landingpages/README.md` in `Itegro-GmbH/itegro-gmbh`). The rules of the repository root `AGENTS.md` apply
too (issues, branches, commit messages, no secrets). This folder is its own pnpm project (not part of `app/`); it
has its own CI workflow (`.github/workflows/website.yml`).

## Copy and product rules

- All copy is English. Exceptions required by law: the German imprint text and the German (binding) versions of the
  privacy policy and the terms of service, which keep an English translation next to them.
- The product is called `code-review.com` everywhere, lowercase also at the start of a sentence, never "Code Reviewer".
  Render copy through `inline()` from `src/lib/inline.ts`, so it never breaks at the hyphen.
- code-review.com is a hosted service only. No page may call the product open source or offer self-hosting, and the
  word "instance" is not used. The AI models of the service come first, an own provider key is the option.
- Never invent numbers, customers, logos, testimonials or legal data.
- The approved copy lives in `src/content/*.ts` (home, faq, pricing, about, nav). Change it there.

## Commands

```sh
pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
pnpm dev                 # http://localhost:4321, no /ingest, no tracking (no key)
pnpm verify              # types, legal Markdown check, build and build check; before every commit
pnpm preview             # build + wrangler dev: like production, with /ingest, _headers, _redirects and the 404
pnpm legal:md            # regenerate legal/*.md after a change to the legal texts
```

Node 24 (`.nvmrc`), pnpm 10.33.0. Use your own port for `wrangler dev` (`--port`), never 3000, 3100, 4319 or 8787.

## Rules

### Legal texts and URLs

- `/imprint/`, `/privacy/` and `/terms/` are linked from the app and from old mails: keep these paths. `/impressum`,
  `/datenschutz` and `/agb` redirect there (`public/_redirects`).
- Change `TERMS_VERSION` in `src/content/terms.ts` whenever the terms change, and tell the app: it stores the version a
  customer confirmed (`BILLING_TERMS_VERSION`). Do not touch the terms text without that.
- The privacy policy describes what `consent.ts`, `analytics.ts`, `cta.ts` and `worker/ingest.ts` do (Klaro cookie
  `code-review_klaro`, full mode with consent, pseudonymous cookieless counting without it, the proxy forwarding the
  IP, the link to itegro-gmbh.de and the hand-over to the app). Change both together, bump `PRIVACY_DATE`, run
  `pnpm legal:md`.

### Tracking (from the template; change only when the process changes)

- PostHog starts only after a decision in the banner. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control count as "Decline".
  Without consent nothing is stored on the device.
- Custom events go through `track()` from `src/lib/analytics.ts` and are sent in full mode only. No other tracker,
  embed or third party script (the CSP in `public/_headers` allows only our own origin).
- `CROSS_DOMAINS` in `src/site.ts`: links to these domains get `ph_did`, `ph_sid` and `ph_ts` when a consented visitor
  opens them. `itegro-gmbh.de` (same PostHog project) and `app.code-review.com` (hand-over; contract with the app:
  `ph_did` and `ph_sid` on `/sign-up` and `/login`). Only our own domains, never third party sites.
- The tracking files `analytics.ts`, `consent.ts` and `worker/` keep the template's German comments on purpose, so a new
  template version stays easy to compare.

### Blog

The blog is scaffolded (`src/content/blog/`, `src/content.config.ts`) but empty, and an empty blog is invisible: no
`/blog/` page, no navigation or footer link, no RSS link, no sitemap entry. It all appears with the first real article
(one Markdown file per article; real dates, a real author, one concrete question per article). No placeholder articles.

### Pages and SEO

- Every page uses `Base.astro` with its own `title` (without " | code-review.com") and `description` (70–160 characters),
  exactly one `h1`, internal links with a trailing slash.
- Images from `src/` with `<Image />` (optimised, with width and height), always with `alt`.
- A new page with its own preview image: add its path to `src/pages/og/[...path].png.ts` and pass `ogImage={ogPath(...)}`.
- No inline scripts (`<script is:inline>`, `onclick=…`): the CSP blocks them. A normal `<script>` in an Astro component is
  bundled as a file and is fine. Scripts are kept to the minimum (menu, video play button, tracking).
- Colours only as the tokens in `src/styles/global.css`.

### Source files

Every `.ts`, `.mjs` and `.css` file starts with `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Proprietary` (the repository rule,
`make fix-spdx-headers` adds it). `.astro` files are not covered by that rule.
